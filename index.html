📖 Usage Guide

How to use AI SpillGuard Pro:

  1. Upload Image: Drag a SAR or satellite image, or click browse.
  2. Wait for Processing: The AI model runs in your browser (typically < 2 seconds).
  3. Review Results: Examine the visual comparison and detection statistics.
  4. Check Alerts: Oil spills exceeding thresholds trigger alerts.
  5. Download: Export overlays, masks, or JSON reports.
  6. Configure: Adjust thresholds and visualization in the sidebar.

Detection Classes:

  • 🛢️ Oil Spill: Primary target for detection
  • ⚠️ Look-alike: Natural phenomena that resemble oil spills
  • 🚢 Ship/Wake: Vessel activity and wake patterns
  • ⬛ Background: Clean ocean surface
